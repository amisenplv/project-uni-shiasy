# Pilot success metrics

Target 40–60 invited ride-hailing drivers after an initial 5–10-person install and data-safety check. Record cohort denominators, withdrawals, failed installs, missing reports, and outages. Thresholds are pilot decision rules.

| Measure | Definition | Provisional target |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | Drivers who add a vehicle and save a real expense within 48 hours of first use, divided by enrolled drivers with observable outcomes. Report enrolled drivers with unknown outcomes separately. | At least 60% |
| Four-week repeat logging | Activated drivers with at least one saved entry on days 22–28 after first use, divided by activated drivers. Report missing outcomes and wait for at least 25 activated drivers before interpreting the result. | At least 35% |
| Quick-add time | Time from opening quick add to confirmed save for a familiar fuel entry during observation. Record incomplete and retried attempts. | Median under 15 seconds. Aim for at least 80% first-attempt completion across 10 attempts and at least five drivers. |
| Safety and distribution | Failed installs or updates, suspected lost or duplicate records, restore failures, and support issues. | Investigate each suspected integrity or cross-account issue before expanding the cohort. |
