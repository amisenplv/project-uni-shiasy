# Personas and jobs to be done

These profiles describe hypotheses to test with real participants.

| Persona | Main job | Likely friction and pilot implication |
|---|---|---|
| Ride-hailing car driver | Capture expenses between shifts and see monthly vehicle costs | Limited time, uncertain connectivity, and several expense types. Primary cohort because repeat logging can be observed in weeks. |
| Motorcycle courier | Log frequent fuel and consumables on a modest Android phone | Distinct vehicle data, maintenance habits, and mobile constraints. Preserve manual vehicle entry and validate this cohort separately later. |
| Van delivery driver | Track maintenance, downtime, and reimbursable costs | Personal and company spending may need separate ownership or reports. |
| Truck operator | Capture high-cost maintenance and manage downtime | Longer routes, inconsistent connectivity, and different service conventions require separate discovery. |
| Private owner | Remember occasional service and annual expenses | Longer intervals make four-week retention harder to interpret. |
| Repair shop or oil-change operator | Help drivers find accurate services, location, and contact details | Upkeep takes time and value is uncertain. Interview about ownership, corrections, consent, and willingness without promising a product. |

Recruit through a real channel in one area and record who declines or cannot install the APK. Compare current workflows such as receipts, notes, memory, spreadsheets, and existing apps before attributing any behavior to the MVP.
